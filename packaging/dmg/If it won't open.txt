Roxora Imager

Install: drag Roxora Imager onto the Applications folder.

On first launch macOS may say the app "can't be opened" or that the
developer "can't be verified". The app is not notarized by Apple yet; this
does not mean anything is wrong with it.

- macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: double-click the app once and click Done.
  Then open System Settings, Privacy and Security, scroll to Security,
  click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password.
- macOS 14 and earlier: in Applications, right-click (or Control-click) the
  app, choose Open, then Open.
- Or run in Terminal: xattr -cr "/Applications/Roxora Imager.app"

This is needed only once.

To avoid the prompt, install from Terminal in one line (the download is
checked against the SHA-256 GitHub publishes for it):

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/roxora-os/imager/main/install.sh | sh
